# Real writing references / 真实写作参考

Two Chinese articles by Limin Ge, with the annotation logs from their editing process.
The author selected these materials for publication on 2026-10-21.

| Reference | Article snapshot | Annotation log |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI 公开了一批数学成果，so did I — the contrast approach | [Chinese article, v5](contrast/article.zh.md) | [Annotations](contrast/annotations.zh.md) |
| Sharing our AI progress in mathematics | [Chinese article, v10](ai-progress-in-mathematics/article.zh.md) | [Annotations](ai-progress-in-mathematics/annotations.zh.md) |

The first article contrasts AI-assisted mathematical computation with the difficulty
of getting an AI-written account to sound like its author. The second uses the form
of a research announcement for satire. Their logs show editorial comments, direct
replacements, factual corrections, and instructions applied to similar passages.

All four files preserve the selected originals byte for byte. They are historical
editing snapshots from October 2026, not newly fact-checked articles or a complete
export of every revision. Numbers, repeated log sections, and references to working
files are retained as recorded. The original Chinese text is not translated.

These are reading references, not runnable article directories: no private briefs,
earlier draft versions, raw annotation JSON, or generated review pages are included.
For a runnable example, use [the fictional demo](../demo/).

## 中文

这里收录葛立民（Limin Ge）于 2026-10-21 明确选定公开的两篇中文正文及其划线记录。

- **《OpenAI 公开了一批数学成果，so did I》反差方案**：正文为 v5，讲 AI 算数学与
  AI 写文章的反差；划线记录包含主线调整、全文同类修改，以及作者原话直接替换。
- **《Sharing our AI progress in mathematics》**：中文正文为 v10，采用研究成果
  发布说明的形式进行讽刺；划线记录展示结尾删改、算力对比和开头结构的修改要求。

四个文件均与选定原件逐字一致，保留当时的数字、重复记录及工作文件引用。

读者可以对照正文与记录，理解作者如何提出修改要求、哪些内容要求原样采用。
